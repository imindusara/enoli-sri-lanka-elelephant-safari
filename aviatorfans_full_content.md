#### All Aviator Classic fan blades are made from natural Paulownia wood, a deciduous hardwood species native to Australasia, China, and other parts of South East Asia . Being a fast grower, Paulownia is light in weight but nevertheless has amongst the highest strength to weight ratios of any wood in the world. It is beautifully evenly grained with silky, satin appearance and the wood dries to a light honey to blond color. Being low in resins and free of volatile oils, it is easily sanded, worked and molded and is tolerant to staining, painting or polishing. In addition to being light and strong, Paulownia has great sound absorption qualities as well as buoyancy, making it a favorite for the manufacture of surfboards due to its high resistance to salt water, musical instruments and for marine construction. The timber used in our blades is made from solid wood giving an overall 45mm thickness. The blades are cut and designed by our master craftsman by hand from these solid timber boards. The result is an incredibly strong and durable solid timber blade which will not warp or crack. The technique of lamination is universally applied when working with timber in order to increase strength and stability. Our Paulownia is sourced from plantation growers and grown in accordance with ecological principles of sustainability and resource utilization.

### The Aviator History...A Walk Down Memory Lane


Established in 1989, in the Port city of Giresun, Turkey, Aviator Classic Fans Co. has a rich history of dedication, innovation, technology, ethical manufacturing practices and environment protection, this strategy has helped the Aviator brand to focus on quality and consistency rather than creating mass markets and capturing the world. We are a company that strongly believes in ethical manufacturing practices, with that in mind we introduced the DC inverter technology drive motors for our fans and developed the use of Paulownia timber to ensure that a single hardwood tree will never be utilized for our manufacturing. We gave priority and invested a lot in green house farming to produce the Paulownia sustainable farming practices. This innovation soon witnessed our drive and committement in safeguarding the global forest reserves whilst encouraging all other manufacturers to follow our foot steps in maintaining good ecological and sustainable manufacturing practices. today we look up at all users of Aviator Classic Fans as contributors to safeguarding our natural resources. We make it a practice to contribute 5% of our sales towards ecological and sustainable development. Since 1989 we have continued with our successful green environment practices and today we have many followers. What started as a small enterprise manufacturing hand-crafted timber fans for the leisure industry has blossomed into a renowned brand name in the world of high-tech, high quality and green technology, architectural designer fan production facilities that has now spread across Australia, Sri Lanka, China, Taiwan, UAE and Canada.


Our humble beginnings trace back to a vision shared by a group of passionate Sri Lankan`s, Australians and Canadians who saw an opportunity to bring a touch of elegance and timeless charm to the world of leisure and entertainment. Armed with meticulous craftsmanship, an unwavering commitment to quality and environment sustainability, and a deep appreciation for classic design, they set out to create fans that would not only serve a practical purpose but also captivate the senses and elevate the ambiance of any space whilst protecting our natural habitats.


As the years passed, Aviator Classic Fans Co. steadily expanded its reach, gaining recognition for its exceptional products and unparalleled attention to detail. We took pride in every fan that left our workshops, knowing that each one was a testament to the skill, dedication, and creative spirit of our team.


Today, our company stands as a testament to the power of perseverance and the rewards of staying true to one's core values. We have grown step by step, carefully and strategically, to become a respected name in the leisure industry, known for our unwavering commitment to sustainability, quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.


At the heart of Aviator Classic Fans Co. is a team of passionate designers, engineers, and visionaries who share a deep-rooted love for timeless design and a relentless pursuit of excellence. Our production process combines the finest materials, advanced manufacturing techniques, and time-honored craftsmanship, resulting in fans that are not only functional but also true works of art.


From the classic, clean-lined aesthetic of our original models to the more contemporary and customized designs we've introduced over the years, our fans have the power to transform any space, whether it's a cozy living room, a chic restaurant, or a grand event venue. Each fan is a masterpiece, crafted to seamlessly blend form and function, making it a true centerpiece that captures the imagination and elevates the overall experience.


As we look to the future, Aviator Classic Fans Co. remains dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what's possible in the world of fan design and ethical manufacturing practices that contributes to protecting our environment and sustainable development. We continue to invest in research and development, exploring new materials, technologies, and design approaches that will allow us to deliver even more exceptional products to our valued customers.


We take great pride in our journey, and we're honored to share our story with you. Whether you're a long-time fan of Aviator Classic Fans Co. or you're just discovering us for the first time, we invite you to explore our collection and experience the timeless elegance and unparalleled quality that have become the hallmarks of our brand.
